// about me

A little about me.

I'm Mohini — a Computer Science & Engineering student at GLA University, Mathura, graduating in 2029. Right now I'm learning Java and DSA, which means a lot of array problems and the occasional "why won't this compile" moment.

I like building things that feel useful — not just code exercises, but actual projects with purpose. When I'm not doing that, I'm usually in some student tech community, either running something or just showing up and talking too much. (Both, often.)

I've always enjoyed explaining things. If I understand something, my first instinct is to figure out how to make it click for someone else. That's part of why I got into communities in the first place — learning is more interesting when it's not just you.

Outside of code, there's writing, creative work, random side interests, and more browser tabs open than I'd like to admit. I'm somewhere at the intersection of builder, communicator, and enthusiastic person who hasn't figured out what to specialize in yet — and I think that's fine.
